Full name is Kullback–Leibler divergence, it is also called relative entropy and I-divergence. It is a measure of how much an approximating probability distribution $Q$ is different from a true probability distribution $P$. Mathematically it is defined as:

$$
	D_{\text{KL}}(P\|Q)=\sum _{x\in \mathcal{X}}P(x)\log \frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}
$$
